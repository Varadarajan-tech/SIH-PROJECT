Key Helplines for Victims

These are the primary national lines referenced by the ASHA escalation matrix — from atrocity reporting to mental-health counselling.

National Helpline Against Atrocities

14566

NHAA — 24×7 toll-free helpline run by the Department of Social Justice & Empowerment for SC/ST atrocity complaints in Hindi, English and regional languages. Complaints get a docket number and are forwarded to State/District authorities. Online it also offers Register Grievance, Register a Rescue and Track Grievance Status services.

24×7 Voice & VOIP Complaint Docket Official portal ↗

Tele-MANAS Mental Health

14416

National tele-mental-health counselling by MoHFW. Trained counsellors in 20+ languages, 24×7, with referral to local mental-health professionals when needed (also 1-800-891-4416).

24×7 Counselling 20+ Languages

Free Legal Aid — NALSA

15100

National Legal Services Authority helpline for free legal advice and representation. Every atrocity victim is entitled to legal aid under Article 39A and the Legal Services Authorities Act.

Quick Decision Guide

Which Number Do I Call?

In moments of stress, choosing the right service can feel overwhelming. This guide helps.

🚨 Someone is in immediate danger

Call 112 first. Do not wait. If it is about self-harm, stay with the person, remove access to means, and keep talking until help arrives.

⚖️ An atrocity has occurred or is threatened

Call 14566 (NHAA) to register a complaint, and 112 if there is any physical threat. Note the docket number for follow-up — you can also register a grievance or a rescue request online on the NHAA portal. Legal aid: 15100.

💙 Feeling overwhelmed, anxious or hopeless

Call 14416 (Tele-MANAS) for counselling, or start the confidential AI-supported chat on this platform. Professionals monitor high-priority conversations.
