Know Your Entitlements

Acts, Rules & Guidelines

A curated reference of legal protections, victim rights, relief provisions and support helplines relevant to victims and complainants of atrocities. This reference powers the guidance provided across the ASHA platform.

Title & Summary Category Year / Status Reference
The Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989
The principal law preventing atrocities against members of SC and ST communities, prescribing stringent punishments, special courts and Special Public Prosecutors. Substantially strengthened by the 2015 Amendment, which added new offence categories (garlanding with footwear, social and economic boycott, offences against women, etc.).
Act 1989 · Amended 2015 DoSJE
The Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955
Enforces Article 17 of the Constitution (abolition of untouchability) and penalises the practice of untouchability in any form, including denial of access to shops, water sources, places of worship and public conveyances.
Act 1955 · In force DoSJE
The Mental Healthcare Act, 2017
Guarantees every person the right to access mental healthcare and treatment (Sec. 18), and presumes severe stress for persons attempting suicide, directing they not be punished and be provided care (Sec. 115) — the statutory basis for ASHA's trauma-informed support approach.
Act 2017 · In force DoSJE
SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Rules, 1995
Operationalise the PoA Act — duties of investigating officers, timelines for investigation and chargesheet, District Magistrate responsibilities and the machinery for relief. Overhauled by the 2016 Amendment Rules.
Rules 1995 · Amended 2016 Rules PDF
Rule 12 — Relief & Rehabilitation Amounts
Prescribes minimum monetary relief for victims, released in phases linked to case milestones (FIR registration, chargesheet and trial conclusion). States/UTs operate victim compensation schemes that top up the central norms.
Rules Rule 12(4) · 2016 Rules PDF
Witness & Victim Support Provisions — 2016 Rules
Travel, food and maintenance allowances for attending investigation and trial; composite relief framework; Protection Officers; State contingency plans for atrocity-prone areas; monitoring by State and district vigilance committees.
Rules 2016 Amendment DoSJE
Section 15A — Rights of Victims & Witnesses
Codified victim rights under the PoA Act: right to justice with dignity, protection from intimidation and harassment, information of rights and case progress, free legal aid, relief and rehabilitation, and compensation. See the cards below for details.
Rights Sec. 15A · PoA Act Act PDF
Right to NGO & Advocate Assistance
Victims and their dependants have the right to take assistance from non-government organisations, social workers and advocates throughout the proceedings.
Rights Rule 12(12) · 2016 Rules PDF
Free Legal Aid — Article 39A & NALSA
The Constitution directs the State to provide free legal aid. The National Legal Services Authority (NALSA) and State/District Legal Services Authorities provide free legal counsel, including to atrocity victims, through the helpline 15100.
Rights Art. 39A · LSA Act 1987 NALSA
National Helpline Against Atrocities (NHAA) — 14566
24×7 toll-free helpline (voice / VOIP) in Hindi, English and regional languages for reporting atrocities against SCs/STs. Complaints are registered with a docket number and forwarded to State/District authorities for action. The NHAA portal also provides Register Grievance, Register a Rescue and Track Grievance Status online services; the helpline operates under the Central Sector Scheme for implementation of the PCR Act, 1955 and PoA Act, 1989.
Helpline 14566 · 24×7 · Toll-free NHAA
Tele-MANAS (National Tele Mental Health) — 14416
24×7 mental-health counselling helpline by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, with trained counsellors in 20+ languages (also 1-800-891-4416). The primary escalation contact for non-emergency psychological support.
Helpline 14416 · 24×7 · Toll-free Tele-MANAS
National Emergency & Support Lines — 112 / 181 / 1098
112 — unified police, ambulance and fire emergency (ERSS); 181 — Women Helpline; 1098 — Childline; 14567 — Elderline; 14446 — National De-addiction Helpline. For imminent danger, always call 112 first.
Helpline 112 · 181 · 1098 Full directory
National Commission for SCs & STs
Constitutional bodies (Arts. 338 & 338A) that investigate and monitor safeguards, inquire into complaints and advise on planning — key escalation and oversight authorities alongside State vigilance and monitoring committees.
Institution Arts. 338 / 338A DoSJE
Special Courts, Exclusive Police Stations & Monitoring Committees
The PoA framework mandates special courts with Special Public Prosecutors, exclusive police stations in identified atrocity-prone areas, and State/district-level vigilance and monitoring committees to review cases and relief delivery.
Institution PoA Act · Ch. IV–V DoSJE

Government Schemes & Empowerment Programmes

Central sector and centrally sponsored schemes compiled from the official DoSJE schemes directory. Recovery after an atrocity is not only legal — these programmes support education, livelihood and dignity.

🏛️ PCR & PoA Implementation Scheme

Central sector scheme (since 1974-75) funding States to implement the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955 and the PoA Act, 1989 — it operates the NHAA helpline (14566) and awareness/relief machinery. nhapoa.gov.in ↗

🏘️ PM-AJAY

Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojna — integrated development of SC-dominated villages, infrastructure grants and hostel construction. pmajay.dosje.gov.in ↗

🎓 Scholarships for SC/OBC Students

Pre-Matric, Post-Matric and Top-Class Education scholarships, National Fellowships and the National Overseas Scholarship — education continuity for children affected by atrocity. scholarships.gov.in ↗

💼 PM-DAKSH Yojana

Free skill-development training for SCs, OBCs, EBCs and DNTs — upskilling and placement support to rebuild livelihoods. pmdaksh.dosje.gov.in ↗

🏦 NSFDC & NSKFDC

Concessional loans and micro-financing for economic development of SCs and safai karamcharis, plus entrepreneurship schemes via NBCFDC. nsfdc.nic.in ↗

🧹 NAMASTE & SEED

Rights of Victims & Witnesses at a Glance

Every victim of an atrocity and their dependants are statutorily entitled to the following protections.

⚖️ Justice with Dignity

Fair treatment with respect for dignity and privacy, without secondary victimisation during reporting, investigation and trial.

🛡️ Protection

Protection from intimidation, threat, harassment or harm by accused persons or their associates throughout proceedings.

📢 Information

Information of their rights, of relief entitlements, and of progress in investigation, chargesheet and court proceedings.

👨‍⚖️ Free Legal Aid

Assistance from advocates and NGOs/social workers, and free legal services from Legal Services Authorities.

💰 Relief & Compensation

Minimum monetary relief under Rule 12 in phased instalments, plus state victim compensation — independent of conviction.

🏥 Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation support including medical care, temporary accommodation, education and livelihood assistance where prescribed.

🗣️ Heard in Proceedings

Right to be heard at appropriate stages and to appear as a witness in court with attendance and travel support.

🚨 Immediate Emergency Relief

Urgent medical and subsistence relief promptly on occurrence of the atrocity, without waiting for investigation to conclude.

Relief & Rehabilitation — How It Works

Understanding the process helps victims and families claim what they are entitled to.

Relief payment process

  • FIR registered — immediate interim relief becomes payable.
  • Chargesheet filed — subsequent instalment of the prescribed relief.
  • Trial concluded — final instalment; states may enhance amounts above central minimums.
  • Relief is in addition to any compensation ordered by the Special Court.

Where to seek help

  • 14566 — NHAA: complaint docket, forwarded to State/District authorities.
  • District Magistrate / SDM — responsible for relief and rehabilitation measures.
  • District Legal Services Authority — free legal counsel (or call 15100).
  • Tele-MANAS 14416 — psychological support alongside the legal process.
